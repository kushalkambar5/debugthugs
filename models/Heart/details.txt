Gliomas: Tumors that occur in the brain and spinal cord, beginning in the gluey supportive cells (glial cells) that surround nerve cells.

Meningiomas: Tumors that arise from the meninges, the membranes that surround your brain and spinal cord. (Often benign, but can cause pressure).

Pituitary Tumors: Abnormal growths that develop in your pituitary gland (a pea-sized organ behind your nose).
