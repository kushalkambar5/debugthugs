The Challenge

Problem Statement & Clinical Vision

HippoHealth
schedule

Diagnostic Delays

Traditional diagnostic reporting for X-rays, MRIs, and ECGs takes hours or days, causing patient anxiety and delayed treatment in critical scenarios.

Wearable smartwatch biometrics (heart rate, sleep, BP, blood glucose) remain isolated on mobile devices without real-time physician integration.
